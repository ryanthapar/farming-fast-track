Why This Method

Pull up the top hundred agents in the GTA. Or just name the top agents you already know in your own market. Then ask what they actually do to get listings — online leads, geographical farming, or both? Look closely at the ones who never seem to run out of listings, and you'll keep finding the same thing: they own a neighbourhood.

“Go to where the decision-maker and the product are — at the door.”

The homeowner and the home, in the same place, at the same time. No other lead source puts you there.

Is This You?

Who This Is For

Geographical farming isn't for every agent — but for the right one, it's the last lead-generation system you'll ever need to build.

First — one thing farming is not

You will never stand at a door and ask someone if they want to sell their home. That isn't geographical farming, and it isn't what you'll be taught. What you'll learn is exactly what to say instead — so the neighbourhood knows you, remembers you, and calls you when it's their time.

You want to own a neighbourhood

Not chase leads all over the city. One area where your name is the one people already know — before they ever think about a brokerage.

You want to be the listing agent

Not the one showing other people's listings every weekend. Farming is how you get on the other side of that.

You want to hold your commission

No discounting, no apologizing for your fee. Step 12 is that exact conversation — the one Monica uses to sign at 6%*, and how to hold your own fee.

You're done driving all over the GTA

One farm, close to home. No listing appointment in Barrie and a showing in Oshawa on the same day.

You want a Canadian coach who's still in the trenches

In person, in the room, with an active REALTOR® who works this market and knows its demographics — not a recorded course from someone who left the business years ago.

You're ready to actually do the work

This is a knock-on-doors business. The system works — and it works when you work it, one step at a time.

You want a business you can one day sell

A farm is an asset. It has a name, a territory, and a repeatable system — so you can build a team on it, scale it, and eventually sell it. A pipeline of bought leads disappears the day you stop paying for it.

You can commit five hours a week

That's the number. Five focused hours in your farm — which leaves the other 35 for online, social media, and your buyers. Farming doesn't replace your business. It anchors it.

And who it isn't for

If you're looking for a lead list to buy, an ad platform to outspend everyone on, or a system that runs without you ever knocking on a door — this isn't that. Farming rewards the agent who shows up in the same neighbourhood, over and over, until it's theirs.

If that's you, you're ready to invest $1,000 + HST in a business that pays it back on your first listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is it, and is parking included?

Venu Event Space, 2800 Hwy 7, Vaughan, ON L4K 1W8. Parking is free and ample on site — no permits, no meters, no hunting for a spot.

Are lunch and refreshments provided?

Yes — lunch is provided. There are also two breaks built into the day, one mid-morning and one mid-afternoon.

What should I bring?

Just your laptop. Your workbook and pen are provided — you'll be building your plan on the page as the day goes.

Do I need to have a farming area picked already?

No. Choosing the right area is step 2 of the day, and Monica walks you through how to do it. It does help to arrive with two or three areas in mind — you'll get more out of that session if you have something concrete to work with.

Is this suitable for newer agents?

Yes. The day is built for brand new agents and seasoned agents alike. Farming doesn't require a database or years of past clients; it requires a defined area and a system, which is exactly what you'll build.

Can teams or brokerage groups attend?

Yes. If you'd like to bring a team or a group from your brokerage, reach out to us about brokerage programs — use the Request More Info form and we'll get back to you.

What materials are included?

Your written 12-step farming plan in order, door-knocking scripts, a flyers and giveaways calendar, plus your workbook and pen. You also get a 1-month membership to Monica's private "No Chai Samosa" group, where farmers share wins, scripts and support.

Can I cancel or transfer my ticket?

No. Tickets are non-refundable and cannot be transferred to another person or another date. If you're not certain the workshop is right for you, book a 15-minute call with Monica before you register — it's better to ask first than to hold a seat you can't use.

How and when do I redeem the bonuses?

We reach out to you after the event to book your private door-knocking session with Monica — that one is redeemable within a year, in Etobicoke. The group Zoom implementation session runs four weeks after the event, and the link is sent to you five days beforehand.

Is a payment plan available?

No — there are no payment plans. Registration is paid in full at checkout: $1,000 + HST, $1,130 in total.
